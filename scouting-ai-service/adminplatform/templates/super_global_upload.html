About Global Data

The global CSV is the shared knowledge base inherited by every academy's chatbot. It contains platform-wide Q&A (registration, rules, general info).

When an academy uploads their own CSV, rows with the same Intent_ID will override the global row. All other global rows are appended to the academy's data automatically.
